About AWS SA

Our history, our mission, and the vision that guides everything we do.

Our story

History

Animal Welfare Society of South Africa (AWS SA) is based in Philippi, Cape Town, where our team has grown into a trusted resource for the surrounding community. What began as a small effort to help animals in need has grown into a full range of services, including veterinary treatment, sterilisation programmes, adoption and community education.

Today, AWS SA continues to support pet owners who might otherwise struggle to access affordable veterinary care, while working to reduce the number of animals living without proper shelter, food or medical attention.

Why it matters

Our approach

We believe animal welfare and community welfare go hand in hand. Rather than turning families away, we work with them: offering affordable treatment, practical advice and sterilisation support so that fewer animals end up without a home in the first place.

Our Mission

To relieve the suffering of animals in the greater Cape Town area through accessible veterinary care, responsible sterilisation, adoption and public education, while treating every animal and every pet owner with dignity and respect.

Our Vision

A Cape Town where every animal is cared for, every owner has access to the resources they need, and animal welfare is understood as a shared community responsibility.

What guides us

Our values

Care

Every animal that comes through our doors receives attentive, professional treatment.

Compassion

We treat animals and the people who love them with patience and empathy.

Responsibility

Sterilisation and education help prevent suffering before it starts.

Community

We work alongside Cape Town families, not apart from them.
