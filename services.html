Our Services

Practical, affordable support for animals and the people who care for them.

Veterinary Care

Our veterinary clinic offers affordable treatment, vaccinations and general health care for the pets of families across Cape Town. We aim to make quality veterinary care accessible to everyone, not just those who can afford private rates.

Enquire about Veterinary Care

Good to know

Open to all Cape Town pet owners, with reduced rates based on need. Walk-ins are welcome, but booking ahead through the enquiry form helps us plan.

Adoption

Every animal in our care is looking for a second chance. Our adoption process is designed to match dogs, cats and other animals with homes that suit their needs, with guidance and support before and after adoption.

Start an Adoption Enquiry

Good to know

All adoptable animals are vaccinated and sterilised before they go home. Our team follows up after adoption to help the transition go smoothly.

Sterilisation Programmes

Uncontrolled breeding is one of the biggest drivers of animal suffering in under-resourced communities. Our sterilisation programmes offer safe, affordable procedures that help manage the local pet population responsibly.

Book a Sterilisation Enquiry

Good to know

Sterilisation is available for both owned pets and community animals, and is offered at both our Philippi centre and through the mobile outreach unit.

Animal Welfare & Education

We run community workshops and share practical resources on responsible pet ownership, animal behaviour and basic animal care — helping prevent welfare issues before they start.

Ask about our Workshops

Good to know

Workshops are run for schools, community groups and individual pet owners, and can be tailored to specific topics on request.
